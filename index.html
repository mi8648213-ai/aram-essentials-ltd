About Us

Professional construction and property solutions.

Aram Essentials Ltd is a private company limited by shares. Its documented business objects include procurement and execution of general contracts, supply of general building materials, construction-related activities, property development, general cleaning, hygiene and drainage services, borehole drilling and general water supply, as well as other stated supply and distribution activities.
